# Recurring Analysis Questions

## CRM

- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue and repeat purchases?
- Which acquisition channels produce high-value RFM customers?
- Which customers are at risk and what actions are associated with recovery?
- Which interaction types correlate with conversion, retention, or churn risk?

## RFM

- How stable are RFM segment assignments across scoring runs?
- Which segments changed most since the previous scoring run?
- Are thresholds producing useful separation in revenue and engagement?
- Which recommended actions should map to each RFM segment?

## AI Agent

- Which AI workflows are adopted most often by sales or operations users?
- Which workflows have the lowest task success rate or highest latency?
- Where do tool calls, retrieval, or model responses fail most often?
- Do AI-assisted customer interactions improve follow-up speed or conversion?

## RAG

- Which knowledge collections are retrieved most often?
- Which documents produce low-confidence or ungrounded answers?
- Are retrieval failures caused by missing documents, stale indexing, or query mismatch?
- How often are citations accepted, ignored, or corrected?
